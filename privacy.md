# Privacy Policy

<p class="meta">Effective date: May 12, 2026</p>

This is the privacy policy for **tanksalot**, an iOS app developed by Dean Silfen (Silfenco). Contact: **<a href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}">{{ site.contact_email }}</a>**.

## Data we collect

**None.** tanksalot does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data off your device.

The app stores tank inventory and session history locally using Apple's SwiftData framework and an App Group container shared with the widget extension. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly back it up via iCloud or iTunes as part of your normal iOS device backup, which is controlled entirely by you and Apple.

## Third-party services

**None.** tanksalot uses no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party services of any kind.

## Notifications

The app schedules **local** notifications only — for example, when a tank is predicted to be empty. These are scheduled and delivered entirely on-device. No push notification servers are involved, and no push notification identifiers leave your device.

## Live Activities

Live Activities (the lock-screen countdown and Dynamic Island display) run on-device. No remote push updates are used to drive them.

## Children's data

tanksalot is commonly used by a parent or caregiver to track a child's portable oxygen tanks. Because the app collects no data and transmits no data, no children's information is collected, stored, or transmitted by tanksalot or its developer. COPPA-style disclosures are not applicable, but we want to call this out explicitly given the use case involves minors.

## Account information

There are no accounts. You do not sign up, sign in, or provide any identifying information to use tanksalot.

## Changes to this policy

This policy is versioned in git alongside the rest of the marketing site. Material changes will update the effective date at the top. After major app updates, it's worth re-checking this page.

## Contact

For privacy questions, email **<a href="mailto:{{ site.contact_email }}">{{ site.contact_email }}</a>**.
