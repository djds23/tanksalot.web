Know which tank to grab. At a glance.

tanksalot tracks the portable oxygen tanks at home, yours or a loved one's. It estimates how much is left in each one from the fills and sessions you log, so you can see at a glance what's likely full, what's empty, and about how long the tank in use will last. There are no sensors, so the numbers are only as accurate as the data you keep.

Get on TestFlight How it works

Built for the moment you reach into the closet

If you manage portable oxygen at home, for yourself or someone you care for, you've got a closet of tanks to keep straight: which one to grab next, how long it'll last at the prescribed flow rate, and when to reorder. tanksalot keeps a running estimate so you're not doing that math in your head.

Multi-tank inventory

Track as many tanks as you own. Each has a size (M2 / M4 / M6 / M9 / D / E / custom liters), an optional label, a status (Full / Available / Low / Empty / In Use), and a session history. Filter and sort to surface what's actually usable.

Custom sizes and flow presets

Ships with the common M-series and steel cylinder capacities. Settings lets you add your own tank sizes and flow presets (0.5 LPM, 2 LPM, 5 LPM), so the start-session sheet matches your actual prescription instead of a generic picker.

Live Activity countdown

Once a session is running, iOS keeps a live countdown on your lock screen, in the Dynamic Island, and in StandBy. The ring tracks the current tank's estimated remaining percent and the timer counts down to the predicted empty time. The activity flips to a red "Replace tank" state automatically when that moment passes.

Home-screen widgets

Small and medium widgets show the default tank's estimated remaining percent and a one-tap Start button. The large widget lists the whole inventory with capacity bars. During an active session, the widget shows a live countdown that ticks every second.

And the rest

  • Estimates, not measurements tanksalot can't read your tanks directly. Every number is an estimate based on the sizes, fills, and sessions you enter, so it's only as accurate as that data. Keep it current, keep spares on hand, and check your actual supply before you rely on it.
  • Live session, estimated capacity A session is "tank in use at X LPM until ended." From that, the app estimates the remaining liters, percent, and a predicted empty time, updating as the session runs.
  • Predicted-empty notifications Local notifications fire at the predicted-empty moment. No push servers, no account, no internet required.
  • Log past sessions Forgot to start a session? Add it after the fact with start, end, and flow rate. Capacity catches up immediately.
  • Mark Full after refill One tap to reset a tank to its full capacity when it comes back from the supplier.
  • Privacy by design Everything is on-device. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no network calls. See the privacy policy.
