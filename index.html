Know which tank to grab. At a glance.

tanksalot keeps tabs on portable oxygen tanks at home, so you always know what's full, what's empty, and how long the active tank will last — whether the tanks are yours or a loved one's. Remaining capacity is calculated from real usage — no sensors, no guessing.

Get on TestFlight How it works

Built for the moment you reach into the closet

Managing portable oxygen at home — whether for yourself or someone you care for — means juggling a closet of M-series tanks and knowing, often in the dark, which tank to grab next, how long it will last at the prescribed flow rate, and when to start a refill. tanksalot turns that mental math into a glance.

Live Activity countdown

Once a session is running, iOS keeps a live countdown front-and-center — lock screen, Dynamic Island, and StandBy. The ring tracks the current tank's remaining percent and the timer counts down to predicted-empty. The activity flips to a red "Replace tank" state automatically when that moment passes.

Home-screen widgets

Small and medium widgets show the default tank's remaining percent and a one-tap Start button. The large widget lists the whole inventory with capacity bars. During an active session, the widget shows a live countdown that ticks every second.

Multi-tank inventory

Track as many tanks as you own. Each has a size (M2 / M4 / M6 / M9 / D / E / custom liters), an optional label, a status (Full / Available / Low / Empty / In Use), and a session history. Filter and sort to surface what's actually usable.

Custom sizes and flow presets

Ships with the common M-series and steel cylinder capacities. Settings lets you add custom tank sizes and custom flow presets — 0.5 LPM, 2 LPM, 5 LPM — so the start-session sheet matches your actual prescription instead of a generic picker.

And the rest

  • Live session, calculated capacity A session is "tank in use at X LPM until ended." The app shows live remaining liters, percent, and predicted empty time.
  • Predicted-empty notifications Local notifications fire at the predicted-empty moment. No push servers, no account, no internet required.
  • Log past sessions Forgot to start a session? Add it after the fact with start, end, and flow rate. Capacity catches up immediately.
  • Mark Full after refill One tap to reset a tank to its full capacity when it comes back from the supplier.
  • Built for international use Volumes and flow rates use the system locale, so European users see 1,5L and 0,5 LPM automatically.
  • Privacy by design Everything is on-device. No accounts, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, no network calls. See the privacy policy.
